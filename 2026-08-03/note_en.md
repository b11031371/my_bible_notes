## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Ezekiel 34:25-31

**25** "I will make a covenant of peace with them, and cause wild beasts to cease from the land; and they will dwell safely in the wilderness and sleep in the woods.

**26** I will make them and the places all around My hill a blessing; and I will cause showers to come down in their season; there shall be showers of blessing.

**27** Then the trees of the field shall yield their fruit, and the earth shall yield her increase. They shall be safe in their land; and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I have broken the bands of their yoke and delivered them from the hand of those who enslaved them.

**28** And they shall no longer be a prey for the nations, nor shall beasts of the land devour them; but they shall dwell safely, and no one shall make them afraid.

**29** I will raise up for them a garden of renown, and they shall no longer be consumed with hunger in the land, nor bear the shame of the Gentiles anymore.

**30** Thus they shall know that I, the Lord their God, am with them, and they, the house of Israel, are My people," says the Lord God.

**31** "You are My flock, the flock of My pasture; you are men, and I am your God," says the Lord God.

---

## II. Key Summary

- God promises to make a covenant of peace with His people, removing wild beasts from the land so they can dwell safely and lie down in the wilderness and the woods.
- God promises to make the area around His hill a blessing and send rain in its season, so the trees will yield fruit and the ground will yield its produce; the people will no longer be prey for other nations, devoured by wild animals, or suffer famine and disgrace.
- God says His people will know that the Lord is with them, that they belong to Him as His flock and His people, and that He is their God.

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### The things that hurt you — God says He'll take them away, one by one

> The covenant of peace speaks of far more than just the absence of war or conflict — it's a person's whole state being well: body, surroundings, and relationship with God, all whole. [1]

The passage then lists exactly what God removes: wild beasts gone, the yoke of slavery broken, no more plunder or shame from foreign nations, no more hunger (vv26-29). God doesn't tell His people to just tough it out on their own — He says He will deal with these harmful things Himself, one by one.

---

### You belong to God, and that doesn't change

> "These are men... that I am a God to, and will be so for ever." [2]

The passage ends with, "You are My flock, the flock of My pasture; you are men, and I am your God" — God Himself settles this relationship, and makes clear it's forever, not something decided by how well you perform.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

Ezekiel 34 describes how God personally shepherds the flock — seeking the lost and healing the wounded (v16), leading them to good pasture (v14), protecting the sheep from harm (v25, 28). In your life right now, is there someone, or something, caring for you in one of these ways?

Whatever state you're in, the passage says God promises to shepherd you in these concrete ways, until you can lie down in safety.
