## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Ezekiel 48:15-22

**16** These shall be its measurements: the north side four thousand five hundred cubits, the south side four thousand five hundred, the east side four thousand five hundred, and the west side four thousand five hundred.

---

## II. Key Summary

- The five thousand cubits in width that remain are for general use by the city, for dwellings and common-land, with the city in the center; each side of the city measures four thousand five hundred cubits, with two hundred and fifty cubits of common-land on every side (15-17).

- The rest of the length alongside the holy district runs ten thousand cubits east and ten thousand west, and its produce is food for the workers of the city; the workers of the city, from all the tribes of Israel, shall cultivate it (18-19).

- The entire district set apart, together with the property of the city, is foursquare, twenty-five thousand cubits by twenty-five thousand; the land left on either side belongs to the prince, and the holy district with the sanctuary of the temple is in the center (20-22).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### The workers come from every tribe

> The city itself is no longer, as of old, to belong to any particular tribe, but is to be situated on the common oblation, and its labourers are to be taken alike from all the tribes. Thus the old jealousies are to be extinguished, and in this, as in all other respects, each tribe is to be treated like every other. [1]

Verse 19 says the workers of the city come "from all the tribes of Israel," and verse 18 says the produce of that land is food for those workers. The workers come from every tribe, and not one tribe is skipped.

---

### When all the measuring is done, the holy place is in the middle

> The real point of all these descriptions is to give a central focus, and that focus is the holy district — more precisely, the holy place (the sanctuary) set at the very center of that central area. [2]

Verse 15 says "the city shall be in the center," and verse 21 says "the sanctuary of the temple shall be in the center." The previous section (48:1-14) measured out the land for the tribes and for the priests and Levites; this section (48:15-22) measures out the city and the prince's land — and when all the measuring is done, what keeps ending up in the middle is the holy place.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

What do you think God looks at first when He picks someone for His work?

In this passage the workers in the city come "from all the tribes of Israel" — God set it up that way when the land was divided; the door into Christ's kingdom stands open to all who believe, and whoever will may come freely.
