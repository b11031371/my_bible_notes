## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV (verse-by-verse summary):** 1 Chronicles 11:1–9

**1** All Israel gathers around David at Hebron and recognizes him as one of their own people.

**2** They remember that David led Israel even during Saul’s reign, and that God had appointed him to shepherd and rule Israel.

**3** Israel’s elders meet David at Hebron. They make a covenant before the Lord and anoint him king, fulfilling the word given through Samuel.

**4** David and all Israel go to Jerusalem, then called Jebus, where the Jebusites live.

**5** Although the inhabitants insist that David cannot enter, he captures Zion’s stronghold, later called the City of David.

**6** David promises leadership to whoever attacks first. Joab goes up first and becomes commander.

**7** David settles in the stronghold, so it becomes known as the City of David.

**8** David builds around the city beginning at the Millo, while Joab restores the remaining area.

**9** David continues to grow in power because the Lord of hosts is with him.

---

## II. Key Summary

- Israel’s elders covenant with David at Hebron and anoint him king in fulfillment of God’s word through Samuel.
- David leads Israel in capturing the Jebusite stronghold, and Joab becomes commander after attacking first.
- David settles in and builds the City of David; the passage says he grows stronger because the Lord is with him.

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### God Keeps His Word Through a Long Wait

> “God is faithful to His people and keeps His promises.” [1]

David’s anointing was not a last-minute idea. It completed what God had spoken through Samuel years earlier, showing that a long wait does not mean God has forgotten his word.

---

### Real Strength Comes From God’s Presence

> “He came to the place of greatness because the LORD of hosts was with him.” [2]

David acted, fought, and built, but the passage points beyond his skill to God’s presence as the reason he kept growing stronger. Courageous action matters, yet it is not the same as moving forward with God.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

When someone says you cannot do it, how do you want to take your next step?

The Jebusites said David could not enter, but God was with him; you can carry that hope as you move forward.
