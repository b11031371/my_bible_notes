## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Ezekiel 39:21–29

**21** "I will set My glory among the nations; all the nations shall see My judgment which I have executed, and My hand which I have laid on them.

**22** So the house of Israel shall know that I am the Lord their God from that day forward.

**23** The Gentiles shall know that the house of Israel went into captivity for their iniquity; because they were unfaithful to Me, therefore I hid My face from them. I gave them into the hand of their enemies, and they all fell by the sword.

**29** And I will not hide My face from them anymore; for I shall have poured out My Spirit on the house of Israel,' says the Lord God."

---

## II. Key Summary

- God says He will set His glory among the nations so that all of them see the judgment He has carried out; from that day on, the house of Israel will know that the Lord is their God (vv. 21–22).
- God gives the reason Israel went into captivity: they were unfaithful to Him, so He dealt with them according to their uncleanness and transgressions and hid His face from them, handing them over to their enemies (vv. 23–24).
- The Lord God declares that He will bring back the captives, have mercy on the whole house of Israel, be jealous for His holy name, and hide His face no more, because He has poured out His Spirit on the house of Israel (vv. 25–29).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### Why God Hid His Face

> The nations would know that Israel's suffering was never because God was unable to help them. The calamity came upon the house of Israel because of their sin and unfaithfulness. That was why Yahweh hid His face from them. [1]

The exile looked like God had lost, when in fact it was God Himself doing the disciplining. He spells out the reason so that nobody misreads who He is.

---

### The Promise That He Will Not Hide Again

> The indwelling of the Spirit is an infallible pledge of the continuance of God's favour. He will hide his face no more from those on whom he has poured out his Spirit. [2]

God's guarantee here isn't "try harder next time" — it's His own Spirit placed inside them. The passage ends by putting the weight of restoration on God, not on their performance.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

When you've done something wrong and you feel awful about yourself, what do you think God's attitude toward you is right now?

In Ezekiel, God says He hid His face because of sin — and then promises, "I will not hide My face from them anymore," because He has poured out His Spirit.
