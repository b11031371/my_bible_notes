## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** 1 Chronicles 16:37-43

**37** So he left Asaph and his brothers there before the ark of the covenant of the LORD to minister before the ark regularly, as every day's work required;

**38** and Obed-Edom with his sixty-eight brethren, including Obed-Edom the son of Jeduthun, and Hosah, to be gatekeepers;

**39** and Zadok the priest and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of the LORD at the high place that was at Gibeon,

**40** to offer burnt offerings to the LORD on the altar of burnt offering regularly morning and evening, and to do according to all that is written in the Law of the LORD which He commanded Israel;

**41** and with them Heman and Jeduthun and the rest who were chosen, who were designated by name, to give thanks to the LORD, because His mercy endures forever;

**42** and with them Heman and Jeduthun, to sound aloud with trumpets and cymbals and the musical instruments of God. Now the sons of Jeduthun were gatekeepers.

**43** Then all the people departed, every man to his house; and David returned to bless his house.

---

## II. Key Summary

- David left Asaph and his brothers before the ark in Jerusalem to serve the LORD regularly, doing each day's work; Obed-Edom, Hosah, and sixty-eight brethren were assigned as gatekeepers.
- David assigned Zadok and the priests to the tabernacle of the LORD at Gibeon to offer burnt offerings every morning and evening as the Law commanded; Heman, Jeduthun, and others chosen by name played trumpets, cymbals, and instruments to give thanks to the LORD, "because His mercy endures forever."
- Once everything was set, the people went home, and David returned to bless his own household.

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### Worship Is an Every-Day Thing

> The worship of God is not only to be the work of a solemn day now and then, brought in to grace a triumph; but it ought to be the work of every day. David therefore settles it here for a constancy, puts it into a method. [1]

The day the ark arrived was a huge celebration, but David didn't let worship stop there—he set people in place to keep it going "as every day's work required." Loving God isn't measured by how hyped you feel on one day, but by whether it keeps going every day.

---

### Worship Goes Home With You

> David returned to bless his house, resolving to keep up family worship still, which public worship must not supersede. [1]

After the big event, David didn't treat it as "mission accomplished"—he went home and blessed his family. Faith isn't only for church or camp; home and ordinary life are where it really takes root.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

Think back to the day after your last Sunday service or church camp, once you were back home: from waking up to going to bed, what did you do that day?

David had people serve before the ark "as every day's work required," and then he went home to bless his family—worship isn't meant to stay at the gathering, but to follow you into every ordinary day, because God's mercy is there every day too.
