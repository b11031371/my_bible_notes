## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Psalm 119:89–104

**89** Forever, O Lord, Your word is settled in heaven.

**90** Your faithfulness endures to all generations; You established the earth, and it abides.

**91** They continue this day according to Your ordinances, For all are Your servants.

**92** Unless Your law had been my delight, I would then have perished in my affliction.

**93** I will never forget Your precepts, For by them You have given me life.

**94** I am Yours, save me; For I have sought Your precepts.

**95** The wicked wait for me to destroy me, But I will consider Your testimonies.

**96** I have seen the consummation of all perfection, But Your commandment is exceedingly broad.

**97** Oh, how I love Your law! It is my meditation all the day.

**98** You, through Your commandments, make me wiser than my enemies; For they are ever with me.

**99** I have more understanding than all my teachers, For Your testimonies are my meditation.

**100** I understand more than the ancients, Because I keep Your precepts.

**101** I have restrained my feet from every evil way, That I may keep Your word.

**102** I have not departed from Your judgments, For You Yourself have taught me.

**103** How sweet are Your words to my taste, Sweeter than honey to my mouth!

**104** Through Your precepts I get understanding; Therefore I hate every false way.

---

## II. Key Summary

- The psalmist says God's word is settled in heaven forever, that God's faithfulness endures to all generations, and that the earth and all things continue this day according to God's ordinances as His servants (vv. 89–91).
- The psalmist says that if God's law had not been his delight, he would have perished in his affliction; and though the wicked wait to destroy him, he will still consider God's testimonies (vv. 92–95).
- The psalmist says he meditates on God's law all day long, and so has more wisdom than his enemies, more understanding than his teachers, and more insight than the aged; he says God's words are sweeter than honey in his mouth (vv. 97–103).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### God's Word Doesn't Change When Everything Else Does

> "Jehovah's word is not fickle nor uncertain; it is settled, determined, fixed, sure, immovable. Man's teachings change so often that there is never time for them to be settled; but the Lord's word is from of old the same, and will remain unchanged eternally." [1]

When the psalmist says God's word is "settled in heaven," the point is that it doesn't shift just because things on earth shift. What's popular and what everyone says changes every year, but what God has said doesn't expire because of it.

---

### What You Keep Thinking About Becomes Your Wisdom

> "What we love we love to think of; by this it appeared that David loved the word of God that it was his meditation. He not only read the book of the law, but digested what he read in his thoughts. It was his meditation not only in the night, when he was silent and solitary, and had nothing else to do, but in the day, when he was full of business and company; nay, and all the day; some good thoughts were interwoven with his common thoughts, so full was he of the word of God." [2]

The psalmist understood more than his teachers and the elders not because he read faster, but because he let God's word stay in his head and kept turning it over, even when he was busy. Whatever you replay in your mind all day eventually becomes the way you judge things.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

What one thing has your mind kept coming back to today?

The psalmist says the thing that made him wiser than his enemies and his teachers was the words he kept turning over in his mind all day long.
