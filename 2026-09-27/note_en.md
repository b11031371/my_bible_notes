## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** 1 Chronicles 16:1-6

**1** So they brought the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tabernacle that David had erected for it. Then they offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.

**2** And when David had finished offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.

**3** Then he distributed to everyone of Israel, both man and woman, to everyone a loaf of bread, a piece of meat, and a cake of raisins.

**4** And he appointed some of the Levites to minister before the ark of the Lord, to commemorate, to thank, and to praise the Lord God of Israel:

**5** Asaph the chief, and next to him Zechariah, then Jeiel, Shemiramoth, Jehiel, Mattithiah, Eliab, Benaiah, and Obed-Edom: Jeiel with stringed instruments and harps, but Asaph made music with cymbals;

**6** Benaiah and Jahaziel the priests regularly blew the trumpets before the ark of the covenant of God.

---

## II. Key Summary

- The people brought the ark of God into the tent David had set up for it, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before God.
- After the offerings, David blessed the people in the name of the Lord and gave every Israelite, man and woman, a loaf of bread, a piece of meat, and a cake of raisins.
- David appointed Levites to serve before the ark—to commemorate, thank, and praise God—with Asaph leading on cymbals, others on harps and stringed instruments, and the priests Benaiah and Jahaziel regularly blowing trumpets.

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### "Commemorate" Means Helping People Remember

> Simply remembering God's great works is an important and often neglected part of the Christian life. [1]

The word "commemorate" in verse 4 literally means "to call to mind." David gave some people one special job: keep everyone from forgetting what God had done—because it's easy to enjoy the party and forget who threw it.

---

### Not Just One Big Day

> "Regularly" does not mean nonstop; it means a fixed service that is repeated on a regular schedule. [2]

The day the ark arrived was a huge celebration, but David didn't let worship end there—he set up a team to serve before God on a steady schedule. A relationship with God isn't kept alive by one emotional moment, but by coming back to Him again and again.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

David appointed Levites to "commemorate" before the ark—to help everyone remember what God had done. Looking back on this week, what is one thing you remember God doing for you? When and where did you think of it?

David didn't let worship stop at one exciting day; he had people remember and give thanks every day, so the whole nation would be reminded daily that God was right there among them.
