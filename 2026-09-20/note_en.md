## II. Key Summary

- While David was hiding at Ziklag out of fear of Saul, and later at the stronghold in the wilderness, fighting men kept coming over to him — including Benjamites from Saul's own tribe, men of Gad, and men of Manasseh.

- When men of Benjamin and Judah arrived at the stronghold, David first went out and asked whether they came peaceably to help him or to betray him; the Spirit came upon Amasai, who answered, "We are yours, O David... For your God helps you," and David received them and made them captains.

- The passage closes by recording that at that time men came to David day by day to help him, until it was a great army, like the army of God.

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### The help people brought was signed by God

> In Amasai's song the words "help" and "peace" appear again and again, answering the very "help" and "peace" David had asked about — proof that it was God who supplied all this "help" in the middle of David's weakness. [1]

David asked whether these men came to help him or to hand him over (v. 17), and Amasai's answer lifted the question one level higher: "For your God helps you" (v. 18). The same word "help" keeps returning in verses 1, 17, 18, 19, 21 and 22, pointing past the helpers to the One behind them.

---

### Men with nowhere to go became an army like the army of God

> Under the hand of God and His servant David, these mighty men — who began as disaffected people with nowhere else to go (1 Samuel 22:1-2) — developed into an amazing force. [2]

Verse 22 says they came "day by day to help him, until it was a great army" — this army was added up one day at a time. What they started with was not strength but having nowhere else to turn.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

In the past month or two, who went out of their way to help you with one specific thing? Lending you something, walking with you, speaking up for you — all of it counts. Write down the person and what they did.

When David was at Ziklag and most afraid, people came to help him day by day, and the passage traces the source of that help back to God — "For your God helps you."
