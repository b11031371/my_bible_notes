## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Ezekiel 48:1-14

**1** "Now these are the names of the tribes: From the northern border along the road to Hethlon at the entrance of Hamath, to Hazar Enan, the border of Damascus northward, in the direction of Hamath, there shall be one section for Dan from its east to its west side;

**2** by the border of Dan, from the east side to the west, one section for Asher;

**3** by the border of Asher, from the east side to the west, one section for Naphtali;

**4** by the border of Naphtali, from the east side to the west, one section for Manasseh;

---

## II. Key Summary

- God divides the land starting from the far north — Dan, Asher, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, Reuben, and Judah each receive one section, every tribe getting a full strip running from east to west (vv. 1-7).
- Next to the border of Judah, a "district" is to be set apart for the Lord, twenty-five thousand cubits wide and the same length as the other portions, with the sanctuary located in its center (vv. 8-10).
- This holy district belongs to the priests of the sons of Zadok, who the text says did not go astray when the children of Israel went astray; the Levites' land beside it may not be sold or exchanged, because it is holy to the Lord (vv. 11-14).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### The Sanctuary Placed at the Center

> The center of the land of Israel is the holy district, and God's sanctuary is located at the center of that district — showing that the Israel to come is a spiritual community centered on God. [1]

This long stretch of measurements looks like dry surveying data, but the arrangement itself is the message: God did not put the sanctuary in a corner or along the edge. He put it dead center. He isn't asking for a slot in your life — He's asking for the middle one.

---

### Holding On When Everyone Else Drifts

> Ministers faithful to the Lord in the worst of times; and who are and will be taken notice of by him, both now and hereafter. [2]

Verse 11 singles out the sons of Zadok because they "did not go astray... as the Levites went astray" — when the people around them drifted one by one, they kept what God had charged them to keep. The point is that God remembers who stood firm in that moment, and He answers that faithfulness with the place closest to Himself.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

List out what you actually spent the most time on and thought about most this week — which one sits right in the middle?

In the land Ezekiel saw, the sanctuary stood at the exact center — God has never been asking for a slot in your life, but for the middle one.
