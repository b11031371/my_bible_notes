## I. Today's Reading

**10** Therefore David blessed the LORD before all the assembly; and David said: “Blessed are You, LORD God of Israel, our Father, forever and ever.

---

## II. Key Summary

- David blessed the LORD before all the assembly, calling Him “LORD God of Israel, our Father.” He said that the greatness, power, glory, victory, majesty, all that is in heaven and earth, and the kingdom belong to God, and that riches and honor come from Him (vv. 10-12).
- David thanked God and asked, “Who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly as this?” He said all things come from God and “of Your own we have given You,” and that they are aliens and pilgrims before God, with days on earth like a shadow (vv. 13-16).
- David said God tests the heart and has pleasure in uprightness; he had offered willingly with an upright heart and had seen with joy the people offer willingly. He asked God to keep this forever in the hearts of the people, and to give Solomon a loyal heart to keep God's commandments and build the temple (vv. 17-19).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### Even the Willing Heart Comes from God

> “David knew that both the ability and the heart to give were themselves gifts from God. He was actually humbled by having such a heart to give, both in himself and in the people of Israel as a group.” [1]

In verse 14 David asks, “Who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly as this?” Then he answers his own question: “For all things come from You.”

As the commentary reads it, David counts even the willing heart among the things that come from God.

---

### Asking God to Keep That Heart

> “For David knew that unless God would ‘fix their heart toward You,’ even if people turn to God and follow Him for a while, in the end they cannot last.” [2]

In verse 17 David says he has seen with joy the people “offer willingly”; in verse 18 he goes on to ask God to “keep this forever in the intent of the thoughts of the heart of Your people, and fix their heart toward You,” and in verse 19 he asks for “a loyal heart” for Solomon.

In a moment of joy, David asks God to keep that heart in place.

## IV. Reflection Question

If you had just given your own allowance or time to help someone, and everyone was praising you, what would you do?

David and the people had just willingly offered great amounts of gold and silver, and David blessed the LORD before all the assembly, saying, “All things come from You, and of Your own we have given You.” (v. 14)
