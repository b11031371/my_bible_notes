## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Amos 9:7-15

---

## II. Key Summary

- The Lord asked Israel, "Are you not like the people of Ethiopia to Me?" He brought Israel up from Egypt, and also the Philistines from Caphtor and the Syrians from Kir; He would destroy the sinful kingdom from the face of the earth, yet not utterly destroy the house of Jacob (vv. 7–8).
- The Lord would sift the house of Israel among all nations as grain is sifted in a sieve, yet not the smallest grain would fall to the ground; the sinners who say, "The calamity shall not overtake nor confront us," would die by the sword (vv. 9–10).
- On that day the Lord would raise up the fallen tabernacle of David; the days are coming when the plowman shall overtake the reaper, the captives will return, rebuild the waste cities, plant vineyards, and be planted in their land, never again to be pulled up (vv. 11–15).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### "Are you not like the people of Ethiopia to Me?"

> The Israelites thought that because God had brought them out of Egypt, they enjoyed a special status; but in God's eyes they were the same as the Cushites, who had no such experience. [1]

The sinners in verse 10 say, "The calamity shall not overtake nor confront us," leaning on exactly this special status; but the Lord speaks of the Exodus in the same breath as the Philistines from Caphtor and the Syrians from Kir (v. 7).

Having come out of Egypt did not mean calamity could not overtake them.

---

### "On that day I will raise up the tabernacle of David"

> Without this last passage and change of tone, the book of Amos would be incomplete. "It is now declared that the reason of the divine judgment is not revenge, but the only way in which it is possible to usher in the restored order on which the heart of God is set." [2]

Verse 10 still ends with "shall die by the sword," verse 11 opens with "I will raise up," and the last verse says, "no longer shall they be pulled up from the land I have given them" (v. 15).

After the sword, what the Lord wants is to rebuild.

## IV. Reflection Question

Is there something you know is wrong but have kept doing, thinking, "I'm a believer anyway—God will forgive me"?

Because they had come out of Egypt, the Israelites said, "The calamity shall not overtake nor confront us"; yet the Lord saw them as the people of Ethiopia, and said these sinners "shall die by the sword" (vv. 7, 10).
