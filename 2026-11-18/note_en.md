## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** Amos 8:11–9:6

**11** "Behold, the days are coming," says the Lord God, "That I will send a famine on the land, Not a famine of bread, Nor a thirst for water, But of hearing the words of the Lord.

**12** They shall wander from sea to sea, And from north to east; They shall run to and fro, seeking the word of the Lord, But shall not find it.

**13** "In that day the fair virgins And strong young men Shall faint from thirst.

**14** Those who swear by the sin of Samaria, Who say, 'As your god lives, O Dan!' And, 'As the way of Beersheba lives!' They shall fall and never rise again."

**9:1** I saw the Lord standing by the altar, and He said: "Strike the doorposts, that the thresholds may shake, And break them on the heads of them all. I will slay the last of them with the sword. He who flees from them shall not get away, And he who escapes from them shall not be delivered.

**2** "Though they dig into hell, From there My hand shall take them; Though they climb up to heaven, From there I will bring them down;

**3** And though they hide themselves on top of Carmel, From there I will search and take them; Though they hide from My sight at the bottom of the sea, From there I will command the serpent, and it shall bite them;

**4** Though they go into captivity before their enemies, From there I will command the sword, And it shall slay them. I will set My eyes on them for harm and not for good."

**5** The Lord God of hosts, He who touches the earth and it melts, And all who dwell there mourn; All of it shall swell like the River, And subside like the River of Egypt.

**6** He who builds His layers in the sky, And has founded His strata in the earth; Who calls for the waters of the sea, And pours them out on the face of the earth— The Lord is His name.

---

## II. Key Summary

- The Lord God says the days are coming when He will send a famine on the land—not of bread or water, but of hearing the words of the Lord. People will wander from sea to sea and from north to east, seeking the word of the Lord, but they will not find it (8:11-12).
- In that day the fair virgins and strong young men will faint from thirst; those who swear by the sin of Samaria, by the god of Dan, and by the way of Beersheba will fall and never rise again (8:13-14).
- Amos sees the Lord standing by the altar, ordering the doorposts to be struck. Whether people dig into hell, climb to heaven, hide on Carmel or at the bottom of the sea, or go into captivity, none will escape. The Lord God of hosts touches the earth and it melts—the Lord is His name (9:1-6).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### "But of Hearing the Words of the Lord"

> The day is coming when, during the exile, the people will long for "the word of the Lord" that they neglected in their days of prosperity. [1]

In the previous section (8:4-6), people could hardly wait for the holy days to end so they could get back to business; the Lord's word was right there, and they did not listen. In this section, once captivity comes (9:4 also mentions going "into captivity"), they run to and fro looking for it.

When the word was close, no one wanted to hear it; by the time they wanted it, it could no longer be found.

---

### "The Lord Standing by the Altar"

> Before the altar, people could originally receive God's mercy through sacrifice; but the people's sacrifices were hypocritical, so the altar instead became the place where the people were judged. [2]

How their sacrifices were hypocritical shows in the verse just before: they swore by the sin of Samaria, by the god of Dan, and by the way of Beersheba (8:14).

Beside the altar where they could have found mercy, the Lord declared, "He who flees from them shall not get away, and he who escapes from them shall not be delivered" (9:1).

---

## IV. Reflection Question

Think carefully: are there words you don't pay much attention to now, but that you will need someday?

Amos said, "the days are coming": on that day people will run to and fro, seeking the word of the Lord, but shall not find it (8:11–12).
