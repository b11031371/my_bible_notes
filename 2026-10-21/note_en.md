## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** 1 Chronicles 29:1-9

**1** Furthermore King David said to all the assembly: “My son Solomon, whom alone God has chosen, is young and inexperienced; and the work is great, because the temple is not for man but for the Lord God.

**2** Now for the house of my God I have prepared with all my might: gold for things to be made of gold, silver for things of silver, bronze for things of bronze, iron for things of iron, wood for things of wood, onyx stones, stones to be set, glistening stones of various colors, all kinds of precious stones, and marble slabs in abundance.

**3** Moreover, because I have set my affection on the house of my God, I have given to the house of my God, over and above all that I have prepared for the holy house, my own special treasure of gold and silver:

**4** three thousand talents of gold, of the gold of Ophir, and seven thousand talents of refined silver, to overlay the walls of the houses;

**5** the gold for things of gold and the silver for things of silver, and for all kinds of work to be done by the hands of craftsmen. Who then is willing to consecrate himself this day to the Lord?”

**6** Then the leaders of the fathers’ houses, leaders of the tribes of Israel, the captains of thousands and of hundreds, with the officers over the king’s work, offered willingly.

**7** They gave for the work of the house of God five thousand talents and ten thousand darics of gold, ten thousand talents of silver, eighteen thousand talents of bronze, and one hundred thousand talents of iron.

**8** And whoever had precious stones gave them to the treasury of the house of the Lord, into the hand of Jehiel the Gershonite.

**9** Then the people rejoiced, for they had offered willingly, because with a loyal heart they had offered willingly to the Lord; and King David also rejoiced greatly.

---

## II. Key Summary

- King David told all the assembly that his son Solomon, whom alone God had chosen, was young and inexperienced, and that the work was great, because the temple was for the Lord God (v. 1).
- David said he had prepared for the house of his God with all his might: gold, silver, bronze, iron, wood, and all kinds of precious stones; because he had set his affection on the house of his God, he also gave his own special treasure, three thousand talents of gold of Ophir and seven thousand talents of refined silver, and then asked, "Who then is willing to consecrate himself this day to the Lord?" (vv. 2–5)
- The leaders of the fathers' houses, the leaders of the tribes, the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and the officers over the king's work offered willingly gold, silver, bronze, and iron, and whoever had precious stones gave them to the treasury through Jehiel the Gershonite; because they had offered willingly with a loyal heart, the people rejoiced, and King David also rejoiced greatly (vv. 6–9).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### Giving Because of Love

> "David gave all he gave because he loved the house of God. We naturally give to and support that which we love." [1]

In verse 3 David says, "because I have set my affection on the house of my God," and so, over and above all he had prepared for the holy house, he also gave his own special treasure of gold and silver.

His giving started with the affection in his heart.

---

### Willing Giving Brings Joy

> "If people are 'willing to give themselves to the Lord' (v. 5), giving and serving will bring great joy (v. 9)." [2]

In verse 5 David asks, "Who then is willing to consecrate himself this day to the Lord?" and verse 9 records what followed: "Then the people rejoiced, for they had offered willingly … and King David also rejoiced greatly."

Willing giving led to joy.

---

## IV. Reflection Question

If someone asks, "Who is willing to help out today?" what would you do?

David asked the assembly the same kind of question: "Who then is willing to consecrate himself this day to the Lord?" The leaders offered willingly, and "the people rejoiced … and King David also rejoiced greatly" (vv. 5–9).
