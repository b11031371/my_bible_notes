## I. Today's Reading

**NKJV:** 1 Chronicles 29:20-30

**20** Then David said to all the assembly, "Now bless the LORD your God." So all the assembly blessed the LORD God of their fathers, and bowed their heads and prostrated themselves before the LORD and the king.

**21** And they made sacrifices to the LORD and offered burnt offerings to the LORD on the next day: a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs, with their drink offerings, and sacrifices in abundance for all Israel.

## II. Key Summary

- David told the whole assembly to bless the LORD, and they blessed the LORD and bowed before the LORD and the king; the next day they offered a thousand bulls, a thousand rams, a thousand lambs and sacrifices in abundance, and ate and drank before the LORD with great gladness (vv. 20-22).
- They made Solomon king a second time and anointed Zadok to be priest; Solomon sat on the throne of the LORD in place of David and prospered, all the leaders, mighty men and sons of King David submitted to him, and the LORD exalted him exceedingly (vv. 22-25).
- David reigned over all Israel for forty years, seven in Hebron and thirty-three in Jerusalem; he died in a good old age, full of days and riches and honor, and his acts are written in the books of Samuel, Nathan and Gad (vv. 26-30).

---

## III. Passage Analysis

### "The Second Time": The Fight for the Throne Ends

> "The anointing of Solomon and the coronation ceremony were to declare to all the officials and the people: ① the hidden struggle over the succession to the throne had ended; ② Solomon inherited the throne of Israel as the one chosen by God." [1]

Verse 22 says they made Solomon king "the second time," which means he had been anointed once before (the first anointing is recorded in 1 Kings 1:39). Verse 24 makes a point of recording that "all the sons of King David, submitted themselves to King Solomon."

After this second anointing, even David's sons submitted, and the fight for the throne came to an end.

---

### The Throne Always Belonged to the LORD

> "David 'died in a good old age, full of days and riches and honor,' but God's purpose for the kingdom and the temple still continued. For the 'throne' belongs to God, and the 'kingdom' also belongs to God; it is God Himself who rules over all things and drives history forward." [2]

Verse 23 calls Solomon's seat "the throne of the LORD," and verse 25 says "the LORD exalted Solomon exceedingly."

The person on the throne changed, but the throne was always the LORD's.
